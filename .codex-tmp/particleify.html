Capabilities

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Answers about Particleify exports, supported formats, and browser-based particle generation.

What is a particle generator?+

A particle generator turns an image, SVG, or 3D model into thousands of animated points that move in 3D space.

Can I turn an image into a 3D particle animation?+

Yes. Upload an image, tune the particle count, size, color, shape, and motion style, then preview the animation in real time.

Can Particleify export HTML?+

Yes. Particleify can export a ready-to-use HTML file that includes the generated interactive particle animation.

Can I export particle animations as MP4?+

Yes. Use Export MP4 to record the current particle animation and download it as a video.

Does Particleify support GLB models?+

Yes. Particleify supports GLB uploads, so you can turn 3D models into particle-based visuals.

Is Particleify free to use?+

Creating, customizing, and exporting MP4 videos is free. The current lifetime HTML export price is shown securely at checkout.
